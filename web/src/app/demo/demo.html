Rabbit Ears

A 90s TV in the browser. Folders of shows become channels on a curved tube picture, and something is always already on. This TV is yours alone: nobody else can change it.

Your phone is the remote

Scan to control this TV from your phone, or open the remote in a new tab. Enable sound on this tab first: phone buttons cannot unlock its audio.

VCR: play your own videos

Files stay on your device and are never uploaded.

Limits and how loading works
  • Up to 5 videos across 3 channels. Maximum 30 minutes and 500 MB per video; 1 GB total.
  • MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio works best. Other formats depend on your browser.
  • A folder of shows becomes one channel per show; loose files go on CH 00.
  • Loading replaces your previous tapes only after they check out. Keep this tab open to play them.
  • Video contents stay local; titles and channel names are sent so your phone remote can show them.
  • Non-video files are ignored. Very large or deeply nested folders are refused.
