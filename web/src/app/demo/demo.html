VCR: play your own videos

Try your own videos. Files stay on your device and are never uploaded.

Up to 5 videos across 3 channels. Maximum 30 minutes and 500 MB per video; 1 GB total.

MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio recommended. Other formats depend on your browser.

How loading works

A folder of shows becomes one channel per show; loose files go on CH 00. Loading replaces your previous tapes only after validation. Keep this tab open to play them. Video contents stay local; titles and channel names are sent to coordinate your phone remote. Non-video files are ignored. Very large or deeply nested folders are refused.
